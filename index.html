About the game

Riot Control: Europe is an isometric pixel-art tower defence parody. Crowds of protesters march on the parliaments of twelve European capitals, and you, the Ministry of the Interior, hold the steps. It plays in landscape on Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 or later) and on iPhone and iPad. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Common questions

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Everything is inside the app. It never needs an internet connection.

Where are my saves?

The game has three save slots, stored on your device.

Android: if device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android backs up your save slots to your Google account and restores them when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. Android usually makes this backup about once a day, while the phone is charging and on Wi-Fi.

iPhone and iPad: if you are signed in to iCloud, your save slots sync between your devices through your own iCloud account.

The screen does not turn to portrait.

That is on purpose: the game plays in landscape only. Turn your device sideways.

How do I go back or pause?

Use the pause button in the top bar. On Android, the system Back gesture or button also pauses a run or closes the current screen.

  • Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026.

In short

  • The developer does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code in the game.
  • The game works offline and does not contact any server. The developer does not run a server for the game.
  • Your save slots are stored on your device and, depending on your device settings, in your own Google or iCloud account. The developer cannot see them.
  • There are no purchases in the game.

Your saves

To let you continue a campaign, the game saves, for each of its three save slots: which cities you cleared, your best results in each city, and when the slot was created and last played. It also keeps your settings (sound, music, hints) and whether you have seen the tutorial. All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Android

If device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android's backup service may copy your save slots (and nothing else from the game) to your own Google account, so they can be restored when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Android, not the game, makes and restores that copy under Google's terms and privacy policy. It belongs to your Google account and the developer cannot see it.

iPhone and iPad

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps a copy of your save slots in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they sync to your other devices with the same Apple Account. That copy belongs to your iCloud account; the developer has no access to it.

App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store are provided by Google and Apple under their own terms and privacy policies. If you choose in your device settings to share diagnostics with app developers, the store may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics that it has made anonymous. The game itself does not include any analytics.

Your choices

  • The game works fully without a Google or iCloud backup; your saves then stay on your device only.
  • Uninstalling the game removes the saves stored on that device.
  • To remove the backup copy on Android, delete the game's backup in your Google account (Android settings, Google, Backup). On iPhone and iPad, manage iCloud storage in Settings, your name, iCloud.

Children

The game is not designed for children. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
